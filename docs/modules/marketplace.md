# Global Marketplace

The Marketplace module provides a comprehensive trading platform allowing users to buy, sell, and trade items across a network of connected servers.

## Key Features
- **Cross-Server Trade Feed**: Subscribed servers can host a live feed of all active market trades.
- **User Dashboard**: A central location for users to view and manage their own active listings.
- **Personal Alerts**: Get notified when items you're looking for hit the market.

## Commands

### Prefix Commands
- `,market`: Opens your personal trading dashboard to manage active listings.



## Listing Management
- Users can post trades with detailed "Giving" and "Looking For" information.
- Listings automatically expire to ensure the marketplace remains current.
